Privacy Policy

How this website handles your personal information — UK GDPR compliant.

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, and protected when you visit this website. This website is operated by an individual based in the United Kingdom and this policy is written in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Please read this policy carefully. By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Who Is the Data Controller?

The data controller for this website is the individual operator of Calibrated Ideas, based in the United Kingdom. For any privacy-related enquiries, please email hello@calibratedideas.com.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (a service of GitHub, Inc., a subsidiary of Microsoft). GitHub acts as a data processor on behalf of this website for hosting purposes. Their privacy practices are governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement.

2. What Personal Data We Collect

2.1 Email Contact

This website does not include a contact form. If you choose to email us directly at hello@calibratedideas.com, the following information may be collected:

  • Email address — so we can reply to your message
  • Message content — the enquiry or feedback you provide

Contacting us by email is entirely optional. You are never required to provide personal data to access the meditations or any other content on this site.

2.2 Automatically Collected Data (Server Logs)

When you visit any website, your browser automatically sends certain technical information to the server hosting it. GitHub Pages may collect standard server log data, which can include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and the pages you visit. This is handled by GitHub under their own privacy policy and is not directly accessible to or controlled by this website's operator.

2.3 Data We Do Not Collect

This website does not:

  • Use cookies of any kind (advertising, tracking, or analytics)
  • Use third-party analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics)
  • Place tracking pixels or fingerprinting scripts
  • Require account creation or registration
  • Collect payment information
  • Knowingly collect data from children under 13

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we must have a lawful basis for processing personal data. We rely on the following:

  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR) — when you email us, it is in both your interest and ours to be able to respond to your message. We have assessed that this interest is not overridden by your rights.

4. How We Use Your Data

Personal data received via email is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry, question, or feedback
  • Follow up on any request you have made

Your data will never be used for unsolicited marketing, sold to third parties, or shared with any organisation without your explicit consent, except where required by law.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

Personal data received via email will be retained for no longer than 12 months from the date of receipt, or until the purpose for which it was collected has been fulfilled, whichever is sooner. After this period, data will be securely deleted.

If you request deletion of your data before this period, we will action that request promptly (see Your Rights below).

7. International Data Transfers

GitHub's infrastructure may involve the transfer of data outside the UK or European Economic Area (EEA). GitHub is certified under appropriate data transfer frameworks and implements standard contractual clauses to ensure adequate protection. For full details, see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Beyond GitHub's hosting, no personal data collected through this website is transferred outside the UK.

8. Cookies

This website does not set any cookies itself. GitHub Pages, as the hosting provider, may set minimal technical cookies as part of its infrastructure. These are not advertising or tracking cookies and are not within the control of this website's operator.

As no cookies are set by this website, no cookie consent banner is required under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR). Should this change, this policy will be updated accordingly.

9. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As a data subject under UK GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to any personal data we hold about you:

Right of Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification

Ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.

Right to Erasure

Request that we delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").

Right to Restriction

Ask us to restrict how we use your data in certain circumstances.

Right to Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Right to Object

Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at hello@calibratedideas.com. We will respond within one calendar month as required by UK GDPR. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

There is no charge for exercising your rights unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive.
